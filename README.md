## 💡 What dcabacktest Actually Does

"DCA" stands for **Dollar Cost Averaging**. It is the simple habit of investing a fixed amount of money on a regular schedule — for example, $50 every week — instead of trying to guess the perfect moment to buy.

dcabacktest takes that idea and lets you replay it against real historical crypto price data. You choose the rules, the software shows you what the outcome would have been.

You can explore questions like:

- What if I had bought $100 of Bitcoin every Monday for the last four years?
- Would buying weekly have beaten buying monthly?
- What happens to my result if I skip purchases during price spikes?
- How different is a Bitcoin-only plan from a mixed Bitcoin and Ethereum plan?
- What was my best and worst stretch along the way?

Instead of guessing, you get a clear picture of the journey — including the uncomfortable parts, which are usually the most useful part.

## 🧭 A Simple Walkthrough for First-Time Users

If you have never used backtesting software before, here is a gentle path through the app:

1. **Pick your asset.** Choose Bitcoin, Ethereum, or a blend of both.
2. **Set your budget.** Enter the amount you would realistically invest each time — even a small figure works fine for testing.
3. **Choose your schedule.** Weekly is a good starting point because it smooths out price swings.
4. **Select a time period.** Choose how far back you want the test to run. Longer periods give a fuller picture, including both good and bad market phases.
5. **Run the test.** The software calculates the result and displays a summary along with a chart of how your portfolio would have grown.
6. **Change one thing at a time.** Adjust the schedule or the amount and run it again. Comparing small changes is where the real insight comes from.

There is no wrong way to experiment. Nothing you do inside the application touches real money or a real exchange account.
